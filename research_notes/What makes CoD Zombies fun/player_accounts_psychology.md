## What do players say is the most fun part (friends, tension, box, Easter eggs, atmosphere)

### Takeaway
Across forum and press coverage, the dominant stated draw is playing with friends, followed by shared discovery (Easter eggs, secrets) and nostalgia. Evidence is broad-sentiment, drawn from search summaries, not read first-hand.

### Cited Findings
- Paraphrase of forum/comment results: one player says they would rather kill zombies with friends or even strangers than solo; another says Zombies was a full party of friends playing together; one commenter credits the mode with many friendships and shaping a career path. (anecdote) — [callofdutyzombies.com "What keeps you loving Zombies"](https://www.callofdutyzombies.com/topic/185729-what-keeps-you-loving-zombies), [lemm.ee thread](https://lemm.ee/post/1235428)
- A Digital Trends writer says Black Ops 6 Zombies keeps them hooked by withholding small treats to discover alongside favourite people (paraphrase of the search summary; single writer's view). — [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/?p=3792124)
- A reviewer returning to MW3 Zombies describes playing with the same friends they last fought alongside years ago (nostalgia plus reconnection; anecdote). — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/call-of-duty/how-did-i-not-know-that-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-3-zombies-was-this-much-fun)
- Other recurring favourites in results: Easter eggs, atmospheric distinct maps, open-world design in newer entries. — [Kotaku BO6 impressions](https://kotaku.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-zombies-impressions-xbox-1851681562), [CheatCC "We heart Zombies"](https://cheatcc.com/articles/we-heart-zombies-now-and-forever/)

### Inferences
- Social play is the strongest single motive in the sampled material, with discovery second. The sample is small and biased toward press and forums.

## Mastery, high-round culture, "one more round"

### Takeaway
High-round play is a long-endurance culture: multi-day runs, streamer records, and crashes that erase hours.

### Cited Findings
- Round 100 on Call of the Dead was first reached legitimately in April 2024; a streamer (PromptTheStealthPatcher) reportedly took about 61 hours, using a tactic that divided the community. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/cod-zombies-player-breaks-stunning-call-of-the-dead-record-13-years-later), [Gaming Bible](https://gamingbible.co.uk/games/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-solo-100-call-of-dead-611429-20240405)
- TomPhantasy reportedly reached round 255 on BO3 Nacht der Untoten over 20 days on and off. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/twitch-zombies-record/)
- A BO3 world record was beaten after nearly 300 hours. — [Charlie Intel](https://www.charlieintel.com/zombies-world-record-beaten-after-nearly-300-hours-of-call-of-duty-black-ops-3/116828/)
- A Cold War streamer's world-record run ended by a crash; another attempt was ruined by a server glitch (frustration of no-save long runs). — [Comicbook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-noahj456-zombies-crash), [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/zombies-world-record-attempt)
- Outbreak round 1000 reportedly reached on stream. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/outbreak-level-1000-zombies-record)
- Caveat: a claim of 11,843 rounds came from a low-quality scraper; unverified.

### Inferences
- Records give a public, verifiable mastery goal, and streaming turns endurance into spectator content.

### Gaps
- No player quotes on the "one more round" feeling or training strategies.

## Discovery: Easter eggs and community solving

### Takeaway
Easter eggs are a communal puzzle culture (Discord, Reddit, spreadsheets, streams), sometimes solved years later; developers have since made them easier.

### Cited Findings
- 2021 r/ColdWarZombies post described a Discord of nearly 1,000 members to find players for Easter eggs. — [r/ColdWarZombies mirror](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/ColdWarZombies)
- A BO4 egg was found four years after release; fans still ask about BO3's "impossible" egg (Shadows of Evil) eight years on. — [GGRecon](https://cms.ggrecon.com/articles/an-incredible-black-ops-4-zombies-easter-egg-has-been-found-four-years-later), [GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/articles/cod-fans-want-treyarch-to-reveal-impossible-easter-egg-after-eight-years), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/call-of-duty-zombies-shadows-of-evil-impossible-easter-egg/)
- A fired Treyarch employee leaked BO4 egg solutions on Reddit. — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/black-ops-4-employee-is-fired-reveals-zombie-secrets,288434)
- Opinion piece: data miners turn hunts into a race with legitimate solvers (single opinion). — [Gaming HQ](https://gaminghq.eu/2026/08/22/call-of-duty-zombies-easter-egg-tools/)
- Treyarch reportedly simplified Cold War eggs because under two percent of players saw them. — [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/cod-black-ops-cold-war-zombies-easter-eggs-easier-secrets-ps4-xbox-pc)

### Inferences
- Solving is a social sport, so difficulty and spoiler tools directly shape belonging, not just challenge.

### Gaps

## Gambling and collection (Mystery Box, wonder weapons, GobbleGums)

### Takeaway
General design writing frames box-type rewards as variable-ratio schedules; nothing found studies the Zombies box or GobbleGums.

### Cited Findings
- Variable-ratio reward is the slot-machine schedule. — [Game Wisdom](https://game-wisdom.com/general/invisible-pull-randomized-rewards-mobile-games)
- Yu-kai Chou: a box feels generous when the outcome is bounded (you know you get a weapon, the mystery is which) and exploitative when unbounded; pity systems soften bad pulls. — [Yu-kai Chou](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-analysis/decoding-the-mystery-box-a-dive-into-the-intricacies-of-reward-design/)
- Australian Senate report: most people exposed to variable-ratio gambling do not develop problems, but vulnerable people may. — [aph.gov.au](https://aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Senate/Environment_and_Communications/Gamingmicro-transactions/Report/c03)

### Inferences
- The in-game-currency, bounded box fits the "generous" side; paid or unbounded drops fit the "exploitative" side. This is my application, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No player accounts of the box rush or GobbleGum collecting; no evidence on which feels exploitative.

## Why players quit

### Takeaway
Cited reasons: format changes (MW3 extraction), connection problems, dwindling support, alternatives (Helldivers 2), and loss of ammo/punishing downs.

### Cited Findings
- MW3 players complained the mode differed heavily from earlier wave-based Zombies and felt abandoned. — [Dexerto FR](https://www.dexerto.fr/call-of-duty/joueurs-mw3-abandonnent-mode-zombies-totalement-delaisse-par-developpeurs-1545026/), [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/mw3-players-lament-that-zombies-is-dead-and-theyre-pissed-as-hell-about-it)
- Cold War players furious about connection-interrupted issues; one said they would not buy another CoD. — [Charlie Intel](https://www.charlieintel.com/black-ops-cold-war/cold-war-zombies-players-furious-as-game-breaking-connection-interrupted-issues-continue-120894/)
- CoD veterans leaving for Helldivers 2 for co-op PvE. — [GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/articles/mw3-zombies-fans-are-being-told-to-play-helldivers-2-instead)
- A long-form essay on the "rise and fall" exists but was unreadable. — [Medium](https://medium.com/the-gaming-planet/the-rise-and-fall-of-cod-zombies-5c8cc39add3f)
- Critics say recent easter eggs/early rounds are too easy for veterans. — [Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2024/12/11/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-has-a-major-warzoneification-problem)

### Gaps
- No evidence on monetization, solo boredom, loss of horror, or "died at round 30+ after 2 hours" from sources read.

## Psychology (SDT, flow, player types)

### Takeaway
SDT research shows violence adds little to enjoyment once autonomy, competence and relatedness are satisfied. No horde-mode-specific, flow, or Bartle paper found.

### Cited Findings
- Przybylski, Ryan, Rigby 2009: violent content adds little to motivation once competence and autonomy are accounted for. — [Przybylski et al. PDF](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/2009_PrzbylskiRyanRigby_PSPB-1.pdf)
- Ryan, Rigby, Przybylski 2006: games can satisfy competence, relatedness, autonomy (cited secondhand). — [Reer & Kramer 2018](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/2018_ReerKramer_Psychological.pdf)
- Being able to play the hero and fight bad guys predicts violent-game enjoyment. — [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/the-motivating-role-of-violence-in-video-games/)

### Inferences
- Competence (rounds/records), relatedness (co-op) and autonomy (tactical/route choice) map cleanly to Zombies; my mapping, untested in sources.

### Gaps
- No flow or Bartle sources found; no study of Zombies specifically.
