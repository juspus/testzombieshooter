# Gap-filling round: CoD Zombies eras, mechanics and developer intent (2008-2026)

Method note: 12 WebSearch calls (extended mode), no WebFetch (blocked in this environment). All findings come from search-result summaries, and the source URL listed is the one that summary came from. Sources are mostly fan wikis, forums and guides, so most "reception" items are opinion. FACT versus OPINION is labelled. The earlier files (reception_by_game.md, core_loop_mechanics.md) are not repeated. This round found thin evidence for several gaps, and those are listed under Gaps.

## Black Ops 2 (2012): TranZit, Buried, Grief

### Takeaway
TranZit is widely seen as divisive, with the bus, fog and Denizens named as the main reasons. Treyarch described Grief as a team-versus-team mode. No critic review of Grief or Buried was found; Buried's praise is fan opinion about its features.

### Cited Findings
- FACT (fan description): TranZit's fog obscures vision and is populated by hostile Denizens, which mostly spawn in fog areas and flee when you enter a fog-free area. The bus protects players from Denizens but limits manoeuvrability. — [GameFAQs TranZit info thread](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/669288-call-of-duty-black-ops-ii/64671517); [CoD wiki TranZit](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/TranZit)
- OPINION (Steam guide author): the community "doesn't like it that much", mainly because of the bus mechanic, the fog and Denizens; later forum posts argue both sides, some saying the fog should stay but the Denizens go — [Steam guide, TranZit In-Depth](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=897802526) (search summary of the topic)
- OPINION/THEORY (forum player, not a developer statement): the fog and Denizens were meant to deter players from leaving the main areas — [GameFAQs thread, via search summary](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/669288-call-of-duty-black-ops-ii/64671517)
- FACT (2012 reveal): Treyarch described Grief as two teams of four trying to outlast each other while zombies attack; humans cannot damage each other but can hinder the other team. Also called 4z4 / Last Man Standing by a fan guide — [NBC News, 2012](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/zombies-get-new-life-call-duty-black-ops-2-flna1b6102054)
- FACT (Buried, fan guides): Arthur/Leroy the giant can be freed with a key; Booze makes him charge and break barriers to open routes; Candy makes him smash zombies; the Paralyzer wonder weapon and Time Bomb are map features — [GameRant Arthur/Leroy guide](https://gamerant.com/black-ops-2-buried-arthur-leroy-how-to-free-all-booze-candy-bo2/)
- OPINION (single forum commenter): Buried is "the standout of Vengeance", citing the time rewind bomb, Remington revolver, Paralyzer, and Vulture Aid ("best perk ever") — [Steam BO2 Zombies thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/212910/discussions/0/613938693249370717)
- FACT: Jimmy Zielinski sat down with Call of Duty Elite to discuss Buried (content not read) — [Call of Duty on X, 2013](https://x.com/callofduty/status/350388359916556289?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Complaints cluster on friction between objectives (travelling, fog, Denizens) and the core survive-in-a-room loop; Buried's praised elements are all interactive tools that change how you move or fight. Inference, from forum-grade evidence.

### Gaps
- No critic review of Grief, no player verdict on Grief, no sourced verdict on Buried from a major outlet, no sourced developer statement on why fog/Denizens/bus were made.

## Black Ops 3 (2015): maps, Chronicles, mod tools, GobbleGums

### Takeaway
Blog and forum rankings favour Der Eisendrache and Gorod Krovi and rate Revelations lowest of the DLC maps. BO3 is regarded as very moddable (Steam Workshop). GobbleGum mechanics are documented but design intent and player reaction were not found.

### Cited Findings
- OPINION (one blog's reader-vote table, 2023-ish, methodology unverified): Der Eisendrache 32% of first-place votes, Gorod Krovi 28%, Shadows of Evil 17%, Revelations 4%; ranking puts Revelations sixth of seven ahead of only The Giant — [ExpertBeacon ranking](https://expertbeacon.com/what-are-the-zombie-maps-in-order-black-ops-3/)
- OPINION (blog author): Shadows of Evil has a "super confusing layout" and gumball reliance; Revelations is "a mix of old areas that doesn't fully stand on its own" — [ExpertBeacon](https://expertbeacon.com/what-maps-come-with-black-ops-3-zombies/)
- OPINION (Steam forum commenter): BO3 had better graphics, mechanics and "way more user-generated content" than earlier entries — [Steam BO3 discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/311210/discussions/0/1326718197229078346)
- FACT: Zombies Chronicles is an expansion of 8 remastered maps from World at War, Black Ops and Black Ops II — [Steam Workshop listing](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1665106534)
- FACT (2026 roundup/guide): BO3's mod tools (Radiant Level Editor, APE) are available to all owners and are the same tools Treyarch uses; Steam version only, no console mod support. One guide calls BO3 the easiest CoD to mod because the pipeline is built into Steam — [OpenMods guide](https://openmods.net/guides/getting-started-call-of-duty-black-ops-iii); [Of Zen and Computing custom maps roundup](https://www.ofzenandcomputing.com/best-custom-bo3-zombies-maps/)
- FACT: GobbleGums come as Classic and Mega; Megas (three rarity tiers) are unlocked with Liquid Divinium vials in Dr. Monty's Factory, with better odds in the more expensive vats — [CoD wiki GobbleGum/BO3](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/GobbleGum/Black_Ops_III); official intro: [Call of Duty blog, GobbleGum 101](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/archives/call-of-duty-black-ops-3-zombies--gobblegum-101)
- FACT (patch, Nov 2021): all GobbleGums became unlimited, and offline mode can use Mega GobbleGums — [Steam changelog](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2631943123?p=12)
- OPINION (Steam thread title, content not read): "gobblegum makes this game too easy" — [Steam Workshop discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/1624257987/5943120984227595495)
- FACT/OPINION (Blundell, Kotaku): the BO3 main Easter egg was deliberately made convoluted and elaborate, partly because he was tired of players solving earlier Black Ops secrets quickly; he also said he drew on Miyamoto Musashi's Book of Five Rings when designing an egg meant to last indefinitely — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/cod-blops-3-call-of-duty-black-ops-impossible-easter-egg-2000683922); [GodIsAGeek, 2016](https://godisageek.com/2016/04/treyarch-jason-blundell-talks-call-of-duty-zombies/)
- Blundell categorised Easter eggs as "easy", "hard" and "impossible" (pre-release interview, quoted via a fan blog) — [CoD wiki user blog](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:FromDvToZombies/Zetsubou_no_Shima_pre-release_interview_revealing_a_few_details_about_Jason_Blundell's_thought_proccess_with_DLC_3_and_DLC_4's_Zombies_map.)

### Inferences
- The Easter egg "peak" was an intentional escalation by the design lead (from the Kotaku and GodIsAGeek material), which probably fed both the praise (community puzzle solving) and the later accessibility push (Directed Mode).

### Gaps
- No critic reviews of Shadows of Evil, Zetsubou No Shima, Der Eisendrache, Gorod Krovi or Revelations; no review of Zombies Chronicles; no Treyarch statement on GobbleGum design intent; no source on how GobbleGums "felt" beyond a thread title.

## Black Ops 4 (2018): perk rework, Elixirs, Talismans, Specialist

### Takeaway
BO4 dropped classic Juggernog-style perks for slot-based perks, replaced GobbleGums with Elixirs and added a single Talisman. Health values were later raised. Chaos vs Aether, Specialist reception and default difficulty were not found in citable form.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: Perks sit in named slots (Brew, Cola, Soda, Tonic for Victus/Ultimus; Danu, Ra, Zeus, Odin for Chaos) but the selectable perks are the same; Juggernog, Speed Cola and Double Tap Root Beer are not in BO4 Zombies — [Treyarch blog, "New Zombies Adventures"](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2018-10/new-zombies-adventures); [Screen Rant perk ranking](https://screenrant.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-4-zombies-perks-ranked/)
- FACT: Elixirs are unlimited for basic (green) ones, with stronger ones brewed in the Laboratory menu; Talismans are consumed automatically at round start and only one can be equipped (example: starting with an AR). Official materials list Special Weapon, Elixirs, Perks and Talismans as the customisation pillars — [Treyarch blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2018-10/new-zombies-adventures); [GamesHedge Talismans guide](https://www.gameshedge.com/call-duty-black-ops-4-zombies-mode-talismans-guide/)
- OPINION (Screen Rant): removing Jugger-Nog was "one of the most controversial changes"; some new perks "fell completely flat", others proved surprisingly useful — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-4-zombies-perks-ranked/)
- FACT (reported, weak): a later update raised base health, reported as from 150 to 200 — [HubPages perk explainer](https://discover.hubpages.com/games-hobbies/Treyarch-Updates-Call-of-Duty-Black-Ops-4-Zombies-and-Perk-System-Explained)
- FACT/OPINION (Blundell, Activision blog): the team assumes players are "intelligent and curious" and designs with that in mind; players love cracking a cipher or finding a new technique — [Activision blog, Blundell on BO4 Zombies](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/archives/treyarch-zombies-director-jason-blundell-on-call-of-duty-black-ops-4-zombies)

### Inferences
- Baking survivability into the base kit (slot perks and higher health) trades the buy-Juggernog economic decision for loadout setup before the match.

### Gaps
- Specialist weapon reception, Chaos vs Aether reception, default difficulty and "no Jugger" health numbers from a primary source, and the HubPages health figure should be checked against patch notes.

## Cold War (2020)

### Takeaway
A "too easy" argument exists (one opinion piece), but also reports of high-round difficulty in later games. Rules for exfil are documented; loadout/armor/rarity detail is thin.

### Cited Findings
- OPINION (Lv1 Gaming, c. 2020-21): Cold War Zombies gives the player "way too much power" without obstacles; author says they have everything by round 7, hit round 30-35, exfil, and leave; suggests tougher bosses and exfil every 10 rounds — [Lv1 Gaming](https://lv1gaming.com/call-of-duty-cold-war-zombies-is-too-easy-and-thats-a-problem/)
- FACT: exfil requires reaching round 10 first, then is available every five rounds — [Sportskeeda exfil guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/how-exfil-black-ops-cold-war-zombies)
- FACT (fan/guide): Outbreak's loop is primary objective, side activities, Beacon, then warp/upgrade/exfil; Aetherium Crystals earned from rounds permanently upgrade perks, field upgrades and weapon classes — [Zombies Codex Outbreak](https://www.zombiescodex.com/black-ops-cold-war/outbreak/); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1985810/discussions/0/4695658272798135703)
- FACT: Pack-a-Punch in Cold War has three levels costing 5K, 15K and 30K Essence; salvage can be used to craft equipment, obtain armor and increase weapon rarity — [Blizzard News tips](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23699738/gear-up-for-mauer-der-toten-top-10-tips-for-black-ops-cold-war-zombies); [Wikipedia, Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_Cold_War)
- OPINION (Charlie Intel): many players overlook upgrading Armor Level and Weapon Rarity — [Charlie Intel](https://charlieintel.com/best-weapons-top-tips-to-master-mauer-der-toten-cold-war-zombies/115517/)
- OPINION (Steam, BO6 era): armored zombies have too much health at high rounds to kill fast enough to exfil; solo exfil is harder — [Steam BO6 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1985810/discussions/0/4695658272798135703) (platform id may be mis-tagged; treat as anecdotal)

### Inferences
- Complaints of the form "easy" and "too hard" coexist, mostly separated by game-phase (early power curve versus high rounds), not contradictory.

### Gaps
- No source on "no horror" complaints, armor plate numbers, or rarity tier names; no major-outlet take on "accessible vs too easy".

## Black Ops 6 (2024-25) and Black Ops 7 (2025)

### Takeaway
Pre-release reaction to Citadelle des Morts was strongly positive; GobbleGums return with a cooldown rather than purchase; Directed Mode is framed by Treyarch as newcomer help. The later BO6 maps (The Tomb, Shattered Veil, Reckoning) were not found.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: BO6 Augments give three major and three minor options on Perks, Ammo Mods and Field Upgrades; GobbleGums return with up to five in a pack, free on a five-minute cooldown rather than bought with rising prices — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-zombies-video-details-augments-gobblegums-and-more); [Call of Duty GobbleGum guide](https://www.callofduty.com/guides/zombies/call-of-duty-guides-black-ops-6-zombies-gobblegums)
- OPINION: Augments require grinding to unlock (guide-based, not criticism) — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/how-rank-fast-get-augments-easily-black-ops-6-zombies)
- OPINION (player complaint, Reddit via Dexerto): a Wonderbar GobbleGum kept repeating Ray Guns ("annoying"), 1,000+ upvotes — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/black-ops-6-players-slam-annoying-gobblegum-for-repeat-loot-3058469/)
- FACT: Citadelle des Morts released 5 Dec 2024 (Season One Reloaded) as the third round-based BO6 map, with four wonder weapons (Bastard Swords) not in the box — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-6/season-1-reloaded-citadelle-des-morts-zombies-map)
- OPINION (pre-release): PCGamesN "already won over"; Yardbarker said it could be one of CoD's best maps but would fail if "too convoluted or annoying" — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-6/season-1-reloaded-citadelle-des-morts-zombies-map); [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/video_games/articles/citadelle_des_morts_inside_black_ops_6_zombies_brand_new_map/s1_17458_41342753)
- OPINION: Steam user comments on Citadelle are split ("Very fun" versus "shit map") — anecdotal — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/comments/3377465664)
- FACT (Treyarch framing): Directed Mode brings in newer players, including those who did not follow the storyline, with marked objectives toward the Main Quest; trials and minor Easter eggs are disabled; themed rewards still given. Directed Mode returned in BO7 — [Gaming Intel](https://gamingintel.com/black-ops-6-zombies-guided-save-files/); [Wikipedia, BO6 Zombies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_6_Zombies)
- FACT (Kevin Drew, GamesBeat, BO7): in Directed mode rounds are capped, "it only really gets harder than round 15"; roads link zones because round-based "is very much about that feeling of claustrophobia"; Ashes of the Damned is the largest round-based map Treyarch has made — [GamesBeat, how Treyarch designed BO7 Zombies](https://gamesbeat.com/how-treyarch-designed-black-ops-7s-zombies-interview/) (sources disagree on Directed launch timing, and GamesBeat misnames the map "Ashes of Damnation")
- Kevin Drew (BO6 interview): "I want to bring Zombies to more people" — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/news-black-ops-6-zombies-new-details-revealed-treyarch)

### Gaps
- No reception found for The Tomb, Shattered Veil, Reckoning, or for Augments as a design; no 2026 content.

## Mechanics

### Takeaway
Box and drop rules are documented by fan wikis (unverified against official code). Zombie speed per round, solo revive, door/wall/trap costs and the round-change cue were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- FACT (wiki, general Zombies, not BO1-specific): the teddy bear never appears on the first three box uses; uses four to seven each carry a 15% chance; with the box at its starting location it always appears after eight uses; the bear means the box moves; the player is refunded (950 in later titles) — [CoD wiki Mystery Box](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Mystery_Box); [GameFAQs BO1 thread on refund](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/960188-call-of-duty-black-ops/57232393)
- FACT (fan wiki, Project Lazarus, may not equal official): each killed zombie rolls a 2% chance to try a drop; drops are capped at 4 per round; there is a points requirement (team points) for an attempt, and a failed attempt does not deduct points; zombies must die in the playable area and be killed by a player — [Project Lazarus Power-Ups](https://project-lazarus.fandom.com/wiki/Power-Ups); the 2% figure also appears in [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/960188-call-of-duty-black-ops/60805238?page=1)
- FACT: Carpenter only drops when 5+ barriers are fully destroyed — [CoD wiki Carpenter](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Carpenter)
- FACT (conflicting): hellhound rounds recur about every 5 rounds (CoDZombies Guides: first within rounds 6-8; Neoseeker: every 4-6; Nazi Zombies wiki: every 5 or 6) — [CoD Zombies Guides](https://www.codzombiesguides.com/bestiary/hellhound/); [Neoseeker](https://callofduty.neoseeker.com/wiki/Black_Ops_Zombie_Survival_Guide); [Nazi Zombies wiki](https://nazizombies.fandom.com/wiki/Hellhound)
- FACT (fan compilation): BO1 zombie health at round 5 is 550 — [GameFAQs weapon guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/960188-call-of-duty-black-ops/58266061)
- UNVERIFIED (fan reimplementation, not official): sprinters 3.9-4.5 m/s, walking player 4.1 m/s — [stew-zombies PR 21](https://github.com/michaeljjdougherty/stew-zombies/pull/21)

### Inferences
- The points requirement plus 4-per-round cap suggests drops are a metered economy, not pure chance (inference from the wiki text).

### Gaps
- Official walk/run/sprint round thresholds; the exact hidden points threshold number; door, wall-gun and trap costs; hellhound round-change music cue; solo-revive perk details beyond the earlier file.

## Developer intent

### Cited Findings
- Blundell (BO3/BO4): complex Easter eggs by design; assumes curious players — see sources above.
- Kevin Drew: "I want to bring Zombies to more people" (BO6) and round-based "is very much about that feeling of claustrophobia" (BO7) — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/news-black-ops-6-zombies-new-details-revealed-treyarch); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/how-treyarch-designed-black-ops-7s-zombies-interview/)
- Jimmy Zielinski: Creative/Design Director for Zombies 2010-2014 per his own CV; fan-forum transcript has him describing Easter eggs added to the original mode ("We put even more Easter eggs like that into the game") — [CV](http://jimmyzielinski.com/JIMMYZIELINSKICOVERLETTER.pdf); [forum transcript](https://codzombies.forumotion.net/t1144-jimmy-zielinski-interview)
- Blundell on leaving secrets in the game after he is gone — [Detonated](https://detonated.com/jason-blundell-reveals-huge-zombies-secrets-in-2025-details-on-his-departure-more/)

### Gaps
- No Zielinski quote on accessibility versus depth; no direct Treyarch quote tying Directed Mode to design philosophy beyond Drew's round-cap comment; one Grokipedia "accessible but deep" claim was rejected as uncited.
