# Gaps: first-hand player voices on CoD Zombies, and remaining psychology/roguelite gaps

Method note: WebFetch was not used (the brief says it is blocked). 12 extended WebSearch calls were run. The search tool returns an AI-written summary of results, not raw snippets, and it did not surface actual Reddit threads at all (r/CODZombies, r/ColdWarZombies, r/blackops6 never appeared as URLs). Short fragments in quotation marks below are phrases the summary reproduced from Steam threads and similar pages; they are marked "quote (fragment via search summary)" because I could not open the page to confirm the surrounding text. Everything else is "paraphrase". Verdict: this round added only a small amount of first-hand voice. The key gap (Reddit verbatims) remains open and needs a tool that can read reddit.com.

## Verbatim player quotes on what makes Zombies fun

### Takeaway
Only a few fragments were recoverable. They support the earlier finding that friends and Easter-egg discovery are the draw, and add that satisfying splatter-kills and "classic wave" feel are named by commenters. No Reddit "why do you love zombies" thread was retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Quote (fragment via search summary), a Black Ops fan recalls "the attention to detail and all the easter egg hunting in blops zombie maps were a crazy amount of fun" (anecdote; thread not identified by the summary) — search result set at [steamcommunity CoD Zombies group](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/CoDZombies) and [forums.anandtech.com thread](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/heeeere-comes-a-new-cod-game.2225294/page-5) (which page held the quote is not confirmed)
- Paraphrase: a Reddit user in a COD Mobile zombies debate says they want "rounds like normal zombies, not missions"; another replies wave survival has become repetitive and objectives give more reason to play (anecdote; two opposed views; original Reddit URL not returned) — [Sportskeeda COD Mobile Zombies](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/cod-mobile-everything-you-need-to-know-about-zombies-coming-to-the-game)
- Paraphrase: an NBC News piece on zombie games jokes that the appeal is less the creepiness than the satisfaction of blowing the undead apart on screen (2008-era journalism, single writer; not CoD-specific) — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna21352616)
- Paraphrase: fans of other zombie games push back on special or mutated variants ("We want zombies, not monsters" per a commenter on Into the Dead: Our Darkest Days; a Project Zomboid player says such additions ruin the feel). Anecdotes from adjacent games, not CoD — [Into the Dead thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2239710/discussions/0/800059836888788773), [Project Zomboid thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3463857594044980372)

### Inferences
- The "classic zombie, not monster" sentiment is a data point for keeping specials (exploder, spitter, spider) as variations on the shambling horde and not as the main threat. Low confidence: sources are other games.
- The wave-versus-objective split is a genuine disagreement among players (some want pure rounds, some want goals). Both camps are single comments.

### Gaps
- No verbatim Reddit text for "what made old zombies special", "best zombies memory", "zombies isn't scary anymore". Reddit was not returned by search.
- No Steam review of World at War or BO3 with a first-person memory of the mode itself; BO3 reviews found were about co-op and Workshop (see next section).

## Couch co-op, split-screen, Grief, playing with friends late at night

### Takeaway
Evidence on split-screen is mostly technical (2-player on console WaW; PC split-screen rare) and BO3 Steam reviews praise playing with friends. No late-night or couch memory accounts and nothing on Grief reception were found.

### Cited Findings
- Paraphrase: WaW console Zombies was 2-player split-screen only; 4-player was online only; PC lacked official split-screen (one commenter said BO3 is the only PC CoD with split-screen as of 2020), so PC fans used a mod — [GameFAQs thread](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/944198-call-of-duty-world-at-war/answers/18731-how-do-you-do-4-player-splitscreen-on-zombies), [Steam WaW thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/10090/discussions/0/2619338518532154860/), [ModDB splitscreen mod](https://www.moddb.com/downloads/call-of-duty-world-at-war-splitscreen-pc-v10)
- Paraphrase of BO3 Steam reviews: playing with friends is the reason to buy because the mode is co-op; one calls it among the best zombies experiences; one mentions "too much laughter with the boys"; Steam Workshop custom maps (hundreds) are a major draw; a critical review says multiplayer is dead so the game is worth it only if you have friends for Zombies (reviews, anecdote; reviewer identities not given) — [Steam BO3 community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/311210), [UnitedCritics review thread](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/UnitedCritics/discussions/0/3186865924603574040/)
- Quote (fragment via search summary), an itch.io commenter on a zombie game: "as a huge fan of Call Of Duty Zombies me and my brother love this game"; other commenters there ask for split-screen (anecdote) — [itch.io Krusty Zombies comments](https://hawksandwich.itch.io/krusty-zombies/comments)
- Paraphrase: a WaW Steam group post looks for people to play "old zombies; preferably nacht der untoten", and a UE5 fan remake of Nacht der Untoten markets itself on reliving Zombies "exactly how you remembered it in 2008" (nostalgia is itself a product) — [Cod5 Zombies Steam group](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/Cod5Zombies), [Nacht der Untoten remake](https://blakegibbs.itch.io/nacht-der-untoten-remake)

### Inferences
- Friends and the shared screen are the proven draw; for a browser game, a drop-in co-op link or same-screen option would serve the same need. Inference only.
- A remake community existing 15+ years later suggests the early map's simplicity (one room, boarded windows) holds up; no data on why.

### Gaps
- Grief mode: the one search returned nothing about it (the result summary suspected it is not a WaW mode; Grief began in BO2, which I did not verify). No reception data.
- No late-night / sleepover accounts.

## Quit reasons

### Takeaway
Quit reasons found are mostly modern (MW3/BO6/Cold War): progression grind or loss, endless late game, public-lobby dropouts ruining runs, Easter egg tedium, and bugs. Broad sentiment cannot be claimed; these are individual Steam comments.

### Cited Findings
- Quote (fragment via search summary), Steam poster: "honestly the only reason to play zombies is to grind guns and levels" (so a patch removing that ended interest); another: "You lose all your XP, your camo grind becomes meaningless" after a bug; another: "get to level 30 and extract, that's what i do now"; a MW3 player: "Zombies is a snooze fest"; "when somebody leaves i have to leave because the rounds wont count" (all anecdote, MW3 era, threads not individually identified) — [Steam MW3 thread 1](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/discussions/0/4631482569784864289), [thread 2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/discussions/0/4361250442642304043), [thread 3](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/discussions/0/575995078023056650), [thread 4](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/discussions/0/4032476411646438412)
- Quote (fragment via search summary), Cold War Steam thread: "Why does everyone get kicked when someone who is bad at playing zombies, dies and rage quits the match?" and "Cant even get passed wave 20 in public because its always someone dying then rage quitting and poof." (anecdote; losing a run to others' disconnects) — [Steam Cold War thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1985810/discussions/0/4629233576852851989)
- Quote (fragment via search summary), BO3 Steam comments on Easter eggs: "about halfway through it became so tedious i just gave up and quit"; "WAY too many tasks to do that arent even fun"; one suggests it would be easier with a group. A separate thread: a Shadows of Evil step needs 4 players and quitters block completion (anecdotes) — [Steam BO3 comments 2780964226](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/comments/2780964226), [2271346839](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/comments/2271346839), [BO3 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/311210/discussions/0/351659808495848166)
- Paraphrase: BO3 high-round crashes reported (Revelations, a Verruckt strategy); posters disagree on whether XP or rounds are saved — [Steam BO3 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/311210/discussions/0/343789167954823881)
- Paraphrase: BO6 solo lets players pause and save; the most frequent BO6 grievance, per FandomWire, was no pause in multiplayer, with calls to loosen AFK timers; a co-op pause timer was announced for Season 2 — [Wikipedia BO6 Zombies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Black_Ops_6_Zombies), [IMDb news item citing FandomWire](https://m.imdb.com/news/ni65072737)
- Paraphrase: "too easy" for veterans appears as a single Steam comment and a Sportskeeda remark that BO6 is "quite easy for many hardcore players"; the summary notes no consensus that Zombies lost its fear factor, and another Steam post complains of late-round one-shot damage (round 49) — [Sportskeeda BO6 features](https://sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/7-fan-favorite-features-black-ops-6-zombies-needs-bring-back), [Steam BO6 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/discussions/0/4634862623030085962)
- Paraphrase: Treyarch says BO6 aims to make Zombies more approachable ("I want to bring Zombies to more people"); a Treyarch production director worried about franchise fatigue from back-to-back releases — [Sportskeeda BO6 details](https://sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/news-black-ops-6-zombies-new-details-revealed-treyarch)

### Inferences
- Common thread: quitting is driven by lost progress or lost runs (disconnects, bugs, crashes) at least as much by difficulty. For a browser game: save-and-resume or run recovery, and no run-ending dependence on another player's connection (host migration or bot takeover), are justified. Inference from a handful of anecdotes.
- Grind-only motivation (guns, camos) collapses when progression changes; run-based goals should be the core, not unlock grinding.

### Gaps
- No evidence for monetization as a quit reason, solo boredom, overpowered loadouts trivialising, or pre-2021 no-save complaints beyond the BO3 crash thread.

## Flow and "one more round"

### Takeaway
Only generic flow theory was found; nothing specific to horde modes or Zombies.

### Cited Findings
- Paraphrase: flow occurs when skill and challenge match; too easy gives boredom, too hard gives anxiety — [Game Developer, Cognitive Flow](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cognitive-flow-the-psychology-of-great-game-design), [ResearchGate figure, Csikszentmihalyi model](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Csikszentmihalyis-Flow-mode-Mihaly-1997_fig4_263047439)
- Paraphrase: an experimental first-person-shooter study reports that being enjoyably challenged drives enjoyment — [PMC5954478](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5954478/)
- Paraphrase: a 2026 arXiv paper on Soulslikes argues that trivial failure consequences shift attention and weaken intrinsic motivation — [arXiv 2604.15318](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15318)

### Inferences
- Wave scaling is a flow ramp; the Zombies "too easy for veterans" versus "hard for newcomers" split is a classic skill-gap problem, supporting adaptive or selectable difficulty. My application.

### Gaps
- No design writing found tying flow or "one more round" to horde modes specifically.

## Roguelite unlock pacing, Slay the Spire daily, Dead by Daylight

### Takeaway
Developer primary quotes remain scarce. Two usable developer statements: Mega Crit on adding the daily because of Spelunky, and Behaviour on embracing asymmetry. Vampire Survivors developer statements are about prototyping, not unlock pacing.

### Cited Findings
- Paraphrase, Mega Crit lead designer Anthony Giovannetti (PC Gamer interview): the daily was added because other roguelikes such as Spelunky had one; quote fragment via search summary: "we'll add some modifiers, try to mix things up, but we want this to be a mostly hard experience." The daily gives one attempt on a fixed seed — [PC Gamer interview](https://www.pcgamer.com/slay-the-spire-designer-discusses-new-characters-and-card-game-inspirations/), [Wikipedia Slay the Spire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slay_the_Spire)
- Paraphrase (hobbyist reverse-engineering, not developer documentation): Slay the Spire computes the daily locally from one integer, with no server lookup — [Matt Warden](https://mattwarden.com/projects/spire-daily/)
- Paraphrase, Behaviour creative director Ashley Pannell: the choice was to make both sides similar or two completely different games; the studio chose to embrace asymmetry; early prototypes were simple hide and seek that evolved — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-an-asymmetric-multiplayer-horror-experience-in-i-dead-by-daylight-i-)
- Paraphrase, Vampire Survivors creator Luca Galante: his gambling-app background influenced only chest openings; he settled on three to four options per level-up as the right balance; he wanted a small community platform for weekend content — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/vampire-survivors-development-sounds-like-an-open-source-fueled-fever-dream), [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/vampire-survivors-developer-interview/), [Vice/Waypoint](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-vampire-survivors-went-from-obscurity-to-27000-people-playing-at-once/)
- Paraphrase: Hades commentary calls the meta generous and not a grind wall; critics say risk-taking is needed to earn meta currency and one reviewer quit over grind; Hades II players complain of hours of grinding for basic progression. The Hades II Kasavin interview page (In-Game News, 2026) is generic and unverified, so do not cite it as a developer quote — [Steam Hades thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4357872738333128262/?ctp=3), [Play Journal](https://theplayjournal.com/hades-review/), [In-Game News (unverified)](https://www.ingamenews.com/2026/05/hades-2-development-update-supergiant.html)
- Paraphrase: Risk of Rain 2 ties unlocks to in-game challenges (e.g. MUL-T after five Teleporter completions, Engineer after 30 stages) and keeps a fresh-start structure without permanent stat meta; Screen Rant calls artifacts a way to spice up the game after a couple dozen hours — [GamesRadar unlocks](https://www.gamesradar.com/risk-of-rain-2-character-unlock/), [Screen Rant review](https://screenrant.com/risk-of-rain-2-game-review/), [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2024/4/10/news/141539-risk-of-rain-2-reveals-dead-cells-collab-free-devotion-update-seekers-of-the-storm-expansion-details-trailer/)

### Inferences
- Risk of Rain 2's feat unlocks (do X to unlock Y, no stat meta) are the closest sourced model to the earlier "variety not power" recommendation. A daily with one attempt and a fixed seed can be computed client-side from a date integer, which suits a static browser game (Warden's analysis is the evidence).

### Gaps
- No developer quote on avoiding grind from Supergiant, Motion Twin, Hopoo/Gearbox or Poncle. No Dead Cells source. The AUTOMATON Dead by Daylight 2026 interview was only seen as an intro. Dead by Daylight lessons beyond Pannell's framing were not found.
