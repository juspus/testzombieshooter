Night After Night

Players don't come back because nothing gives them a reason. The first run already has everything, most rewards are crosshair colours, and a run can't be won. This plan fixes that first, mostly with content the game already has. The technical work runs alongside it, so the game stays light on phones and cheap to extend.

  • 5of 49devices came back on a second day since 25 Oct.
  • 6of 6guns are already in the Mystery Box on the first run.
  • 7of 11level rewards are cosmetic. Nothing comes after level 20.
  • 2of 6database migrations never reached production. The daily and weekly boards don't work.

Not now, and why

  • Permanent stat upgrades between runsMore HP or damage feels like progress for a week, then makes the waves you've learned trivial and the leaderboard meaningless. This becomes a card only if you pick B on the first call.
  • Themed special wavesYou skipped them on 27 Oct. NEW BEST and the unlock moment give the run some of that shape without changing the waves.
  • CrazyGames and PokiYou skipped them on 27 Oct. Either way, portals judge first-session retention, so they'd come after this plan.
  • Seasonal eventsMarked Discuss on 27 Oct. A calendar of events needs players who are already coming back.
  • 3–4 playersMarked Discuss on 27 Oct. It's still the P2P vs server question, and the network protocol card makes either path easier.
  • Rapier physics, an ECS library, another engineNo problem today that they solve. Moving to another engine was ruled out on 15 June.
  • Skinned or GLTF zombie modelsThat's a new art pipeline. The merge card keeps the lo-fi box look you picked.
  • Switching maps without a reloadACTIVE_MAP is fixed at page load in six files. Worth fixing only when a feature needs it.
  • Quality tiersYou skipped them, and the frame governor covers the GPU side.

How this was measured

  • Player numbers: SQL over public.events on the Supabase project, 25 Oct to 10 Nov 2026. Migrations: the Supabase migration list against supabase/migrations/.
  • XP pace: calcRunXp and levelFromXp from src/progression.js, for runs that clear 3, 5, 7, 10 and 15 waves, assuming 10% specials and 25% headshots.
  • Tech numbers: headless Chromium (SwiftShader) at 1280x720 on e198bc9; raycast timings repeated 100 times on a desktop CPU in a cloud container. A phone CPU is several times slower.
  • Line counts: wc -l on src without tests, on 05aaef9 (26 Oct) and e198bc9.
