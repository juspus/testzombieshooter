Cabin on a Phone

It runs and it shoots. It doesn't yet treat a phone player as a real player. A new player taps SOLO, waits 15 seconds with no instructions, and is never told how to look, fire or board a window. They have no way to pause, and the buttons are smaller than a fingertip. Nearly all of it is cheap to fix.
