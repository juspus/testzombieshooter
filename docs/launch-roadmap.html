      What's wrong right now

      Players

      Almost nobody has played it

      There is no portal listing, no store page and no itch.io page. Some of the 17 players are us: preview and local test runs write to the same table as real players, so the numbers can't be trusted yet.

      Waves 1 and 2

      Money

      It can't take a payment

      The Stripe keys aren't set, so production reports checkout as off. The score-protection and Daily Run database updates were never applied: the leaderboard accepts any score, and the Daily tab says coming soon.

      Wave 0, about an hour of your time

      Quality

      Thirty systems, no playtest

      The last two roadmaps shipped in three days, built by agents and checked in a headless browser at 4 fps with no sound. MEMORY.md marks all of it as not played by hand or heard on speakers yet.

      Waves 1 and 4

      The order, and why

      Switch it on first. It's about an hour of your time, and without it nothing can earn money or keep an honest leaderboard.

      Then play it and tune it before strangers see it. Portals give a new game a small test audience and decide on more traffic from how that audience plays. The first impression has to be the tuned one.

      Then open the doors, and let the numbers pick what comes after. No new gameplay systems until Wave 2 ships. The game has more systems than players.

      1. Say Go on the database updates and I apply them, then test the Daily tab.
      2. Turn on the Supporter Pack with the Stripe keys, test mode first.
      3. Playtest night 30 minutes with a checklist I write.

      What we're not doing

      • New gameplay systems before Wave 2 shipsEvery new system is one more thing to tune and test, and nobody has played the ones we have.
      • Ads during a runPortal ads only on the start screen and after death. None on our own site.
      • Buying trafficPaying for players before the first minute is tuned means paying to lose them.
      • Paid power or random paid rewardsUnchanged: the Supporter Pack is cosmetic, and the Mystery Box takes in-game money only.

      How we'll know it works

      Today's numbers are tiny and partly our own test runs. Wave 1 cleans them up, so the first real baseline comes after it.
