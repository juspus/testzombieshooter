What we collect

If you sign in:

  • Your Google account id and email address (held by Supabase Auth).
  • Your chosen username, scores, lifetime stats and equipped cosmetics.
  • Your purchase record if you buy the Supporter Pack: Stripe session id and amount. No card data; Stripe handles that.

Anonymously, whether or not you sign in:

  • A random id stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Gameplay events: wave reached, deaths, shop and locker opens, and similar. Each event also records device type (desktop or mobile) and a truncated user agent.

If you are signed in, events may be linked to your account id.

Why

Account and purchase data: to run your account and deliver what you bought (contract). Anonymous events: to balance the game and measure how many players find and buy the Supporter Pack (our legitimate interest in improving a small game).

Who processes it

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Region: EU (eu-north-1).
  • Vercel: hosting and server logs.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Google: sign-in.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU under their own safeguards. We don't sell data.

How long we keep it

Gameplay events: 12 months. Account data: until you ask us to delete it. Purchase records may be kept as long as tax or accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, or deletion by emailing [OWNER_EMAIL]. Deleting your account removes your profile and entitlements; your past scores stay on the leaderboard but become anonymous. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and localStorage

We only use functional storage: your sign-in session, game settings, and the anonymous id above. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Opt out of analytics

You can turn off gameplay analytics on this device. It sets a flag in your browser; clearing site data resets it.

Children

Cabin is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above changes.
